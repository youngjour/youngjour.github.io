This comprehensive urban design project involved developing a masterplan for Hue city, Vietnam, as part of a Korean International Cooperation Agency (KOICA) Official Development Assistance (ODA) project during my research internship at Seoul National University.

## Project Overview

Hue, the former imperial capital of Vietnam, required a comprehensive urban development strategy that balanced modernization needs with cultural heritage preservation. This project addressed sustainable urban growth while respecting the city's UNESCO World Heritage status.

## Design Objectives

- **Heritage Preservation**: Protect and integrate UNESCO World Heritage sites
- **Sustainable Growth**: Plan for population and economic development
- **Infrastructure Development**: Improve transportation, utilities, and public services
- **Cultural Continuity**: Maintain traditional urban fabric and lifestyle patterns
- **Environmental Protection**: Preserve the Perfume River and surrounding landscape

## Project Scope

### Masterplan Development
- **Scale**: City-wide comprehensive planning
- **Timeline**: 2015-2016 development phase
- **Area**: Central Hue and expansion zones
- **Population**: Planning for future growth scenarios

### Key Design Components
- **Land Use Planning**: Mixed-use development strategies
- **Transportation Network**: Multi-modal connectivity planning
- **Green Infrastructure**: Park systems and environmental corridors
- **Housing Strategy**: Affordable and cultural housing typologies
- **Economic Development**: Tourism and cultural industry zones

## Design Approach

### Cultural Sensitivity
- Extensive consultation with local communities and stakeholders
- Integration of traditional Vietnamese urban design principles
- Preservation of historical street patterns and building typologies
- Respect for feng shui principles and cultural practices

### Evidence-Based Planning
- GIS analysis of existing urban conditions
- Traffic flow and accessibility studies
- Environmental impact assessments
- Economic feasibility analysis

### Sustainable Development
- Climate-responsive urban design strategies
- Flood management and resilience planning
- Green building standards and guidelines
- Renewable energy integration strategies

## Key Design Features

### Heritage Integration
- **Imperial City Buffer Zones**: Graduated development intensity around heritage sites
- **Traditional Architecture Guidelines**: Contemporary interpretations of Hue architectural styles
- **Cultural Corridors**: Pedestrian networks connecting historical sites
- **View Shed Protection**: Height and design controls preserving historic vistas

### Modern Infrastructure
- **Transit-Oriented Development**: Clustering development around transportation nodes
- **Smart City Elements**: Technology integration for urban management
- **Utility Modernization**: Upgraded water, waste, and energy systems
- **Public Space Network**: Connected system of parks, plazas, and waterfront areas
