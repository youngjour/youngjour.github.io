As Partner Architect at Ar-ge Architects, Inc. since 2024, I lead various architectural design projects that integrate computational design methods with traditional architectural practice, creating innovative and sustainable built environments.

## Practice Philosophy

Our architectural approach combines evidence-based design with creative innovation, leveraging my research background in urban analytics and data science to inform design decisions. This unique perspective brings scientific rigor to architectural practice while maintaining aesthetic and functional excellence.
