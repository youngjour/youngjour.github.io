Current and recent research projects in urban analytics, computational methods, and data science applications for cities.

## Current Projects

**Center for Advanced Urban System** (2024-current)
*KAIST, GS Engineering & Construction Corporation*
AI-based urban system development and smart city technologies. Leading research on spatiotemporal prediction models and urban analytics frameworks for next-generation urban management systems.

**AI-based Contactless Drug Detection Technology** (2024-current)
*Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST)*
Developing machine learning models for public safety applications using computer vision and sensor fusion technologies. Focus on real-time detection systems for urban security applications.

**Multimodal Spatiotemporal Urban Prediction Model Based on Large-Language Model** (2024-current)
*Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST)*
Exploring applications of large language models for urban analytics, including natural language processing of urban data and multi-modal prediction systems that combine text, spatial, and temporal information.

## Recent Projects

**AI-based Airbnb Market Prediction** (2024)
Developed machine learning models to predict monthly, local-level changes in short-term rental markets using publicly available datasets. Achieved 85%+ accuracy in market predictions by integrating transportation, demographic, and economic indicators. Results presented at International Conference on Tourism Research 2025.

**Urban Trajectory Language Modeling** (2023)
Novel approach treating urban mobility trajectories as a language for deep learning analysis. Applied natural language processing techniques to understand human movement patterns, leading to new insights in mobility prediction and urban behavior analysis. Presented at Association of Collegiate Schools of Planning (ACSP '23).

**Smart Transportation System for Daegu City** (2022-2024)
*Participatory Researcher*
AI-based smart transportation system development for Daegu metropolitan area. Involved in traffic prediction, optimization algorithms, and real-time system deployment for urban mobility management.

**WalkON™ Mobile Health System** (2019-2020)
*Leading Researcher, Swallaby, Inc.*
Led data analysis, mobile app design, and development for smartphone-based walking activity recognition system. Integrated with urban park usage studies and public health applications for evidence-based urban design.

## Research Areas

**Urban Analytics & AI**: Machine learning applications for urban systems, spatiotemporal modeling, and predictive analytics for city planning and management.

**Human Mobility & Behavior**: Evidence-based analysis of pedestrian activity, urban park usage, and movement patterns using smartphone data and GPS tracking.

**Health & Environment**: Intersection of public health, physical activity, and urban design. Focus on walking behavior, park usage patterns, and environmental factors affecting urban wellness.

**Smart Cities & IoT**: Internet of Things applications for urban management, sensor networks, and real-time urban monitoring systems.

**Computational Urban Design**: Integration of data science methods with urban planning and architectural design processes.
