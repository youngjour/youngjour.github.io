## Past Projects

**Hue City Urban Masterplan, Vietnam** (2015-2016)
*Research Intern, Seoul National University / KOICA ODA Project*

Comprehensive urban masterplan development for the former imperial capital, balancing heritage preservation with modern development needs. Project addressed sustainable urban growth while respecting UNESCO World Heritage status.

**Key Contributions:**
- GIS analysis and spatial planning for historic city center
- Integration of traditional Vietnamese urban design principles
- Infrastructure planning and transportation network design
- Cultural corridor development connecting historical sites
- Community consultation and stakeholder engagement

**Project Scope:**
- Scale: City-wide comprehensive planning
- Challenge: Modernization while preserving cultural heritage
- Approach: Evidence-based planning with cultural sensitivity
- Outcome: Comprehensive masterplan and implementation guidelines
