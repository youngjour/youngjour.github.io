This research investigates how park usage patterns differ between physically active and inactive populations in Seoul, utilizing data from a mobile health (mHealth) monitoring system to provide evidence-based insights for urban park design and public health interventions.

## Key Findings

- Distinct temporal and spatial patterns in park usage between active and inactive groups
- Environmental factors influencing park walking behavior
- Design implications for creating more inclusive urban parks
- Evidence for targeted public health interventions

## Methodology

- Mobile health (mHealth) monitoring system data analysis
- GPS-based park usage tracking
- Statistical comparison between user groups
- Environmental and behavioral factor analysis

## Implications

- Urban park design recommendations
- Public health policy insights
- Evidence-based urban planning strategies
