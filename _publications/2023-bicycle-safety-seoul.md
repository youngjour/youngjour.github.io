This study examines safety-critical events in bicycle lanes within the Jongno district of Seoul, providing empirical evidence for improving bicycle infrastructure design and urban mobility safety.

## Research Focus

- Identification and analysis of safety-critical events in bicycle infrastructure
- Spatial analysis of accident hotspots and risk factors
- Urban design recommendations for safer cycling environments
- Integration of mobility data with urban planning principles

## Key Contributions

- Comprehensive mapping of bicycle safety incidents
- Evidence-based recommendations for bicycle lane design
- Policy implications for urban mobility planning
- Methodological framework for bicycle safety analysis

## Methodology

- Field observation and data collection
- GIS-based spatial analysis
- Statistical analysis of safety incidents
- Urban design assessment
