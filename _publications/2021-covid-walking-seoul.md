This study examines how walking patterns in Seoul changed during the early phases of the COVID-19 pandemic. Using smartphone-based activity recognition data, we analyzed the temporal and spatial variations in pedestrian activity to understand the impact of public health measures on urban mobility.

## Key Findings

- Significant reduction in walking activity during lockdown periods
- Spatial variations in walking pattern changes across different urban districts
- Recovery patterns of pedestrian activity as restrictions were lifted
- Implications for urban design and public health policy

## Methodology

- Smartphone-based physical activity recognition
- Spatiotemporal analysis of walking data
- Statistical analysis of pre- and post-COVID walking patterns
