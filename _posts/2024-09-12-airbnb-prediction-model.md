---
title: "Developing AI Models for Local Airbnb Market Prediction"
excerpt: "New research on predicting short-term rental market dynamics using public datasets."
date: 2024-09-12
---

Working on an interesting challenge: can we predict monthly changes in local Airbnb markets using only publicly available data? This research, which I'll be presenting at the International Conference on Tourism Research in 2025, attempts to answer that question.

The motivation comes from urban planning needs. Cities worldwide are grappling with the impacts of short-term rentals on housing markets and neighborhoods. Better prediction tools could help policymakers understand and respond to these changes more effectively.

Our approach integrates multiple public data sources - transportation networks, demographic indicators, seasonal patterns, and urban infrastructure data - to create monthly predictions at the neighborhood level. Early results are promising, with accuracy rates above 85%.

What's particularly interesting is how transportation accessibility emerges as a key predictor. Areas with good public transit connections show different Airbnb market dynamics than more isolated neighborhoods. This suggests urban infrastructure decisions have lasting effects on tourism patterns.

The research also highlights the value of open data initiatives. Much of this analysis wouldn't be possible without cities making datasets publicly available.
