Honored to receive the G-School Best Innovation Award for "Spatial Awareness in Deep Learning: Approaches to Integrating Geolocational Attributes in Deep Learning Models" at the KAIST-NYU Young Researcher Day.

This research explores how to better integrate spatial and geographical context into deep learning models for urban analytics. Traditional machine learning often treats spatial data as just another feature, but geographic relationships have unique properties that deserve special consideration.

The work has implications for urban prediction models, mobility analysis, and smart city applications. It's encouraging to see recognition for research that sits at the intersection of computer science and urban studies.

The collaboration with NYU's Urban X program has been invaluable for developing these ideas. Looking forward to pushing this research further and exploring practical applications in urban planning and design.
