I'm excited to announce that I've joined Ar-ge Architects as a Partner Architect. This new role represents a fascinating opportunity to bridge my research background in urban analytics with hands-on architectural practice.

The position allows me to continue my research at KAIST while leading design projects that integrate computational methods with traditional architectural practice. I'm particularly interested in how data-driven insights can inform better design decisions and create more responsive built environments.

This dual role as researcher and practitioner feels like a natural evolution of my work on evidence-based urban design. Looking forward to sharing more about projects that emerge from this unique combination of academic rigor and design creativity.
