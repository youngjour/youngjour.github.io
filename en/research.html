Research

My research holds behavior- and activity-based, evidence-based urban design as a consistent goal, and I have progressively extended the methodologies that support it. During my Ph.D. I became immersed in data science and statistics; at KAIST I expanded into deep learning and the smart city domain; and I have now entered a stage of directly integrating AI agent systems into architectural and urban design.
