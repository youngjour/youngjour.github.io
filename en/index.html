Introduction

Hello. I’m Youngjun Park, an urban researcher and architect.

Does this street actually get people walking? Is this park being used the way it was planned? How does the perception of a neighborhood differ from one person to another?

Chasing questions like these, I have extended my work into smartphone GPS data, urban computing systems, ML/DL, and artificial intelligence. I completed my M.S. in Urban Design and Ph.D. in Architecture at Seoul National University, and conducted research at KAIST’s School of Computing and Urban AI Institute. I am currently co-founder and CTO of ar-ge inc., developing Agentic AI-based architectural and urban design agent systems.

I explore evidence-based urban research and design practice by placing the traditional language of architecture and the analytical tools of data science on the same desk.
