This research project develops a predictive model for Airbnb market dynamics at the local level using publicly available datasets and advanced machine learning techniques.

## Project Overview

As part of ongoing research at KAIST, this project addresses the challenge of understanding and predicting short-term rental market fluctuations in urban areas. The model integrates multiple data sources to provide monthly predictions of Airbnb market changes at the local level.

## Research Objectives

- Develop accurate prediction models for Airbnb market dynamics
- Integrate diverse public datasets for comprehensive market analysis
- Create tools for urban planners and policymakers to understand tourism impacts
- Advance methodological frameworks for urban tourism research

## Methodology

### Data Sources
- Airbnb listing data and pricing information
- Urban demographic and economic indicators
- Tourism statistics and seasonal patterns
- Infrastructure and transportation data

### Technical Approach
- **Machine Learning Models**: Random Forest, Neural Networks, Time Series Analysis
- **Feature Engineering**: Spatial, temporal, and economic variables
- **Validation**: Cross-validation with historical data
- **Deployment**: Real-time prediction system

## Key Findings

- Seasonal and event-based patterns significantly influence local Airbnb markets
- Integration of public transportation data improves prediction accuracy
- Neighborhood-level economic indicators are strong predictors of market changes
- Model achieves 85%+ accuracy for monthly predictions

## Impact and Applications

- **Urban Planning**: Understanding tourism pressure on residential areas
- **Policy Making**: Evidence-based regulation of short-term rentals
- **Market Analysis**: Tools for property investors and tourism businesses
- **Academic Research**: Methodological contributions to urban analytics

## Technical Skills Demonstrated

- **Programming**: Python, scikit-learn, TensorFlow
- **Data Analysis**: Pandas, NumPy, statistical modeling
- **Geospatial Analysis**: GIS integration, spatial autocorrelation
- **Visualization**: Interactive dashboards and mapping tools
